Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026.

What we collect

Small Team Stack is a static site. We do not run visitor accounts, we do not run advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share visitor data — there is no visitor data to sell. Our hosting provider records standard web server logs (IP address, requested page, timestamp) for security and reliability; we do not otherwise combine that with anything that identifies you.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies. If you follow an outbound or affiliate link to a vendor's site, that site may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy — that policy applies once you have left this site, not ours.

Affiliate links

Links that go through a /go/ path on this site are affiliate links to third-party vendors. Clicking one may record, on our own server, which link was clicked and when, so we can tell which pages are useful — this record is not tied to your name, email, or any account, because we do not have accounts. See our affiliate disclosure for how these links work.

If you email us

If you contact us (see /contact), we keep that email only to answer you and to keep a record of correspondence like correction requests. We do not add you to a mailing list from a one-off email, and we do not share your message with anyone outside our own small team.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the same place as everything else — see /contact.
