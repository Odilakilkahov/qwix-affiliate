Software reviews from people who run small teams

Small Team Stack is a small, independent publication covering the software that small teams and small agencies actually use day to day — not the software vendors pay the most to be covered. We are early: this site is run by a small group, not a large editorial staff, and we would rather publish fewer, honest pieces than a large volume of thin ones.

How we make money

Some of the links on this site are affiliate links. If you sign up through one of them, we may earn a commission from the vendor at no extra cost to you. Every page that contains an affiliate link says so before you reach the link, and the full policy is on our disclosure page. A commission never decides the verdict — see how we test for what does.

What we will not do

  • We will not publish a positive review of a tool we have not actually used.
  • We will not hide a product's real limitations to protect a commission.
  • We will not cover wagering products, predatory high-interest lending, or quick-riches schemes — regardless of commission size.
  • We will not fabricate user counts, testimonials, urgency timers, or scarcity claims.

Who this is for

If you are choosing software for a small team or a small agency and want the trade-offs spelled out before you pay, this site is for you. If you are looking for a ranked "best of" list written to please an advertiser, it is not.
